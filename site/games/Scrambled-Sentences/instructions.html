Scrambled Sentences

English game

How to play

  1. The teacher picks a level, then taps a team to make it active.
  2. Tap the word tiles in the correct order to build the sentence. Tap a word in the sentence to send it back.
  3. Press Check. A correct sentence scores points, then the turn passes to the next team.
  4. Easy has a picture hint and a Listen button. Moderate and Hard add a timer and lose a point for each wrong check.

Difficulty levels

LevelAudienceWhat changes
EasyPrimary4-5 word sentences, picture hint, Listen button, no timer, no penalty. 1 point.
ModerateLower secondary8-10 word sentences, mixed tenses, 75 second timer, wrong check costs 1. 2 points.
HardUpper secondary12+ word sentences with clauses, lowercase tiles, 60 second timer, wrong check costs 1. 3 points.

Scores

Use the +1 and −1 buttons to adjust any team. Press Send to Scoreboard to pass scores to the Scoreboard widget.
