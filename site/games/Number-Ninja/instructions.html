Number Ninja

Math game

How to play

  1. The teacher picks a level, then presses Start round for the active team.
  2. Answer as many problems as you can before the timer ends. Easy and Moderate use answer buttons (keys 1-4 also work). Hard uses typing and Enter.
  3. Each right answer scores points and builds your streak. A wrong answer breaks the streak.
  4. When time is up, the next team becomes active.

Difficulty levels

LevelAudienceWhat changes
EasyPrimaryAdd and subtract to 20, times tables 2, 5 and 10. 3 choices, 90 seconds, no penalty. 1 point each.
ModerateLower secondaryTwo-digit add and subtract (negatives possible), times tables to 12, division, fractions of a number. 4 choices, 60 seconds, wrong answer costs 1.
HardUpper secondaryOrder of operations, squares, cubes, brackets. Type the answer, 60 seconds, wrong answer costs 1. 2 points, plus a bonus every 5 in a row.

Scores

Use the +1 and −1 buttons to adjust any team. Press Send to Scoreboard to pass scores to the Scoreboard widget.
