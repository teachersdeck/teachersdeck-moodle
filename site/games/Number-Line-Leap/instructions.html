Number Line Leap

Maths game

How to play

  1. The teacher picks a level, then presses Start round for the active team.
  2. A number line of lily pads appears; only some pads have numbers. Tap the pad where the asked number belongs and the frog leaps there.
  3. A wrong pad shows its number and where the right one was.
  4. When time is up, the next team plays.

Difficulty levels

LevelAudienceWhat changes
EasyPrimaryWhole numbers on lines to 10 and to 20. 90 seconds, 1 point.
ModerateLower secondaryTens to 100, hundreds to 1,000 and tenths from 0 to 1. 2 points, a miss costs 1.
HardUpper secondaryNegative numbers to −10, halves from −5 to 5, and quarters from −1 to 1 (shown as fractions). 2 points, a miss costs 1.

Scores

Use the +1 and −1 buttons to adjust any team. Podium shows the top three, and Send to Scoreboard passes the scores to the Scoreboard widget. Motion in the header turns the animations down or off; the Tiếng Việt button switches the game to Vietnamese.
