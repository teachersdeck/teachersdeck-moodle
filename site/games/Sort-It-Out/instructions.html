Sort It Out

Science game

How to play

  1. The teacher picks a level for the active team.
  2. Tap a card in the tray to select it (it turns gold), then tap the group it belongs in.
  3. Correct sorts turn green and score points. A wrong group shakes the board, shows the right hint, and costs a point at Moderate and Hard.
  4. Tap a sorted card to send it back to the tray if you change your mind.
  5. When every card is sorted, the round is complete and the next team is up.

Difficulty levels

LevelAudienceWhat changes
EasyPrimaryLiving vs. non-living, with a picture on every card. No penalty. 1 point each.
ModerateLower secondarySort animals into the five vertebrate classes: mammal, bird, reptile, amphibian, fish. Text only. Wrong sort costs 1.
HardUpper secondarySort organisms into the five kingdoms: Animalia, Plantae, Fungi, Protista, and Bacteria (Monera). Wrong sort costs 1. 2 points each.

Scores

Use the +1 and −1 buttons to adjust any team. Press Send to Scoreboard to pass scores to the Scoreboard widget.
