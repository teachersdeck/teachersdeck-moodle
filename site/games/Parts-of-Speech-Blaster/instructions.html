Parts of Speech Blaster

English game

How to play

  1. The teacher picks a level, then presses Start round for the active team.
  2. Words drift across space. The banner says which kind of word to hit, for example Tap the verbs! Tap only those.
  3. A right word bursts and scores. A wrong one bounces away, breaks the streak and, from Moderate, costs a point.
  4. The kind of word changes every 15 seconds, so keep reading the banner. The round lasts 60 seconds, then the next team plays.
  5. The words keep moving even with Motion off: that is the game.

Difficulty levels

LevelAudienceWhat changes
EasyPrimaryNouns and verbs, slow words, no penalty. 1 point each.
ModerateLower secondaryNouns, verbs, adjectives and adverbs (watch out for friendly, an adjective). Faster, a wrong tap costs 1.
HardUpper secondaryPronouns, prepositions and conjunctions. Fastest, 2 points each, a wrong tap costs 1.

Scores

Use the +1 and −1 buttons to adjust any team. Podium shows the top three, and Send to Scoreboard passes the scores to the Scoreboard widget. Motion in the header turns the animations down or off.
