Spelling Bee Showdown

English game

How to play

  1. The teacher picks a level, then presses Start round for the active team.
  2. The word is read out automatically. Press 🔊 Hear it again any time you need a replay.
  3. The student spells the word out loud, one letter at a time. The teacher clicks each letter on the on-screen keyboard as it is said (the keyboard on your computer works too). Use ⌫ to fix a mistake and the apostrophe key for words like they’re.
  4. Press Check spelling (or Enter) when the student has finished. A correct spelling scores points and builds the streak. A wrong one breaks the streak and shows the correct spelling.
  5. If a student can’t spell a word, press Pass. It counts as a wrong answer, so the correct spelling is shown and any penalty applies.
  6. When time is up, the next team becomes active.

Options

  • Pictures — the button in the corner of the word card switches pictures on or off (your choice is remembered). Pictures are only available for the Easy words; Moderate and Hard show no pictures either way.

Difficulty levels

LevelAudienceWhat changes
EasyPrimaryShort 3–4 letter words, with a picture if pictures are on. 180 seconds, no penalty. 1 point each.
ModerateLower secondaryCommon 6–8 letter words people often misspell (because, believe, separate…). 150 seconds, wrong answer costs 1. 2 points each.
HardUpper secondarySilent-letter words (knife, subtle, autumn) and homophones (their/there/they’re, its/it’s) given in a sentence so you know which one is meant. 120 seconds, wrong answer costs 1. 3 points, plus a bonus every 5 in a row.

Scores

Use the +1 and −1 buttons to adjust any team. Press Send to Scoreboard to pass scores to the Scoreboard widget.
