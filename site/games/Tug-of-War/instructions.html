Tug of War

Math game

How to play

  1. Tug of War always races your first two teams — add more teams if you like, but only Team 1 and Team 2 hold a rope end.
  2. The teacher picks a level and presses Start tug of war.
  3. Turns alternate: each side answers one question. A correct answer pulls the rope their way. Easy and Moderate use answer buttons (keys 1–4 also work); Hard uses typing and Enter.
  4. At Moderate and Hard, a wrong or missed answer lets the rope slip back toward the other side.
  5. First side to pull the flag all the way to their edge wins the tug of war and a +2 bonus.

Difficulty levels

LevelAudienceWhat changes
EasyPrimaryTimes tables 2, 3, 4, 5. 3 choices, 20 seconds per pull, no penalty. 1 point each.
ModerateLower secondary12× table plus division. 4 choices, 12 seconds per pull, wrong or missed pulls the rope back. 1 point each.
HardUpper secondarySquares, square roots, and largest-factor questions. Type the answer, 10 seconds per pull, wrong or missed pulls the rope back. 2 points each.

Scores

Use the +1 and −1 buttons to adjust any team. Press Send to Scoreboard to pass scores to the Scoreboard widget.
