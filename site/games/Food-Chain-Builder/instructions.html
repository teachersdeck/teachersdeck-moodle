Food Chain Builder

Science game

How to play

  1. The teacher picks a level, then taps a team to make it active.
  2. Tap organisms to build a food chain, starting with the producer. Arrows point from the organism that is eaten to the one that eats it.
  3. Press Check chain. If a link is wrong, the message names it. Tap a link to remove it and everything after it.
  4. Hard adds an energy question: only 10% of energy passes to each next link.

Difficulty levels

LevelAudienceWhat changes
EasyPrimaryThree organisms, scrambled: plant, plant-eater, meat-eater. Roles are shown after a correct chain. 1 point.
ModerateLower secondaryA full food web. Build a valid chain of at least 4 links. Wrong check costs 1. 2 points.
HardUpper secondarySame food web, plus an energy question using the 10% rule. 2 points for the chain, 1 for the energy answer.

Scores

Use the +1 and −1 buttons to adjust any team. Press Send to Scoreboard to pass scores to the Scoreboard widget.
