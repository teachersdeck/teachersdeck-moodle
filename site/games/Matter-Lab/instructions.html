Matter Lab

Science game

How to play

  1. The teacher picks a level, then presses Start round for the active team.
  2. Read the card and tap the answer. After each answer the particles in the beaker move as solid (packed in rows), liquid (sliding) or gas (flying apart).
  3. Each right answer scores and builds a streak. When time is up, the next team plays.

Difficulty levels

LevelAudienceWhat changes
EasyPrimaryIs it a solid, a liquid or a gas? Everyday things. 90 seconds, 1 point.
ModerateLower secondaryWhat happens when it is heated or cooled: melting, freezing, evaporating or condensing. 2 points, a miss costs 1.
HardUpper secondaryThe state of a substance at a temperature, from its melting and boiling points, and the particle model. 120 seconds, 3 points, a miss costs 1.

Scores

Use the +1 and −1 buttons to adjust any team. Podium shows the top three, and Send to Scoreboard passes the scores to the Scoreboard widget. Motion in the header turns the animations down or off; the Tiếng Việt button switches the game to Vietnamese.
