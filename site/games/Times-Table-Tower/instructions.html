Times Table Tower

Maths game

How to play

  1. The teacher picks a level, then presses Start building for the active team.
  2. Every right answer drops a block onto the tower. A wrong answer makes it wobble and costs a heart.
  3. After three misses the tower topples and the turn ends; otherwise it ends when the time is up.
  4. The next team builds its own tower. Who builds the tallest?

Difficulty levels

LevelAudienceWhat changes
EasyPrimaryThe 2, 5 and 10 times tables. 3 choices, 90 seconds, 1 point a block.
ModerateLower secondaryAll times tables to 12 × 12. 4 choices, 1 point a block.
HardUpper secondaryMissing factors (? × 7 = 56) and two-step questions (3 × 4 × 5, 6 × 7 + 4). Type the answer, 2 points a block.

Scores

Use the +1 and −1 buttons to adjust any team. Podium shows the top three, and Send to Scoreboard passes the scores to the Scoreboard widget. Motion in the header turns the animations down or off; the Tiếng Việt button switches the game to Vietnamese.
