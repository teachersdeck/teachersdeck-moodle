Telling Time

English game: telling the time in English

How to play

  1. The teacher picks a level and British or American English, then presses Start round for the active team.
  2. Each question is one of six kinds, mixed:
    • Read it: a clock; pick how to say the time.
    • Set it: drag the clock's hands to the time in words, then Check.
    • Match it: a digital time; pick the words (or the other way round).
    • Hear it: listen (press the speaker to hear it again) and pick the time. Listen for fif-TEEN and FIF-ty.
    • Fix it: a sentence with a typical mistake; pick the right one.
    • Routine: a daily activity and a time; pick the right sentence, with "at" and the part of the day.
  3. The wrong choices are the mistakes learners really make: "three o'clock fifteen", "seven past three", "fifteen o'clock", past and to swapped, the wrong hour with "to".
  4. After a wrong answer the right one is shown and spoken. The VI button shows one line in Vietnamese about the mistake; it only appears when tapped. Press Next to go on.
  5. When time is up, the next team plays.

British or American

British: "quarter past three", "twenty to four". American: "a quarter after three", "twenty to four"; both say "three fifteen". The switch is at the top of the game and is fixed during a round.

Scores

Use the +1 and −1 buttons to adjust any team. Podium shows the top three, and Send to Scoreboard passes the scores to the Scoreboard widget. Motion in the header turns the animations down or off. In StudentDeck the game is played alone: ten questions a round, with stars.
