Fraction Pizza

Maths game

How to play

  1. The teacher picks a level, then presses Start round for the active team.
  2. Each pizza is cut into equal slices; the slices with pepperoni show the fraction.
  3. Answer as many as you can before the time runs out. Each right answer scores and builds a streak.
  4. When time is up, the next team plays.

Difficulty levels

LevelAudienceWhat changes
EasyPrimaryName the fraction with pepperoni: halves, thirds and quarters. 3 choices, 90 seconds, 1 point.
ModerateLower secondaryTap the pizza that shows the same amount as a fraction (equivalent fractions), or the pizza with more. 90 seconds, 2 points, a miss costs 1.
HardUpper secondaryAdd and subtract fractions (the same denominator, or one twice the other). Type the answer as a/b; any equivalent fraction counts. 120 seconds, 3 points, a miss costs 1.

Scores

Use the +1 and −1 buttons to adjust any team. Podium shows the top three, and Send to Scoreboard passes the scores to the Scoreboard widget. Motion in the header turns the animations down or off; the Tiếng Việt button switches the game to Vietnamese.
