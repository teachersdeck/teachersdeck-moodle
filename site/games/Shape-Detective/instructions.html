Shape Detective

Maths game

How to play

  1. The teacher picks a level, then presses Start round for the active team.
  2. A shape draws itself. Answer the question about it: tap an answer, or at Hard type the number and press Enter.
  3. A right answer brings out the magnifier; a wrong one shows the answer.
  4. When time is up, the next team plays.

Difficulty levels

LevelAudienceWhat changes
EasyPrimaryName the 2D shape: circle, oval, square, rectangle, triangle, pentagon, hexagon, octagon, rhombus, trapezium. 90 seconds, 1 point.
ModerateLower secondaryKinds of angle (acute, right, obtuse, straight), 3D shapes, and how many lines of symmetry. 2 points, a miss costs 1.
HardUpper secondaryArea and perimeter of rectangles, right triangles and L-shapes, in cm and cm². 120 seconds, 3 points, a miss costs 1.

Scores

Use the +1 and −1 buttons to adjust any team. Podium shows the top three, and Send to Scoreboard passes the scores to the Scoreboard widget. Motion in the header turns the animations down or off; the Tiếng Việt button switches the game to Vietnamese.
