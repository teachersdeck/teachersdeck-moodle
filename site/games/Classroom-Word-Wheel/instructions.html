Classroom Word Wheel

English game

How to play

  1. The teacher picks a level. A hidden word or phrase appears, with its category above it.
  2. The active team presses Spin the wheel (or the space bar). Where it stops decides the turn:
    • +1 to +5: pick a consonant. The team scores that many points for every time the letter appears, and spins again. A letter that isn't there passes the turn.
    • Free spin: spin again.
    • Lose a turn: the next team plays.
    • Bankrupt (Moderate and Hard): the team's score goes back to 0 and the next team plays.
  3. Vowels are bought instead of spinning: free at Easy, 1 point at Moderate, 2 at Hard. A vowel that isn't there passes the turn.
  4. Solve it: the team says the answer out loud. The teacher presses They solved it! for the solve bonus, or Not right to pass the turn. Revealing the last letter also counts as solving it.
  5. New puzzle starts the next one with the next team. Letter keys on your keyboard work too.

Teacher tools

  • Own phrase: type your own word or phrase (and a category) to play instead of the built-in ones.
  • Tap a hidden tile to reveal that letter as a hint. No one scores for it.

Difficulty levels

LevelAudienceWhat changes
EasyPrimarySingle words and short phrases (animals, colours, school, food). No Bankrupt on the wheel, vowels are free, solve bonus 3.
ModerateLower secondaryEveryday phrases (greetings, routines, places). Vowels cost 1 point, solve bonus 4.
HardUpper secondaryIdioms and proverbs. Vowels cost 2 points, solve bonus 5.

Scores

Use the +1 and −1 buttons to adjust any team. Podium shows the top three, and Send to Scoreboard passes the scores to the Scoreboard widget.
