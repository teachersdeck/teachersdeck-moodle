Word Match-Up

English game

How to play

  1. The teacher picks a level. Cards are dealt face down.
  2. The active team turns over two cards. If they belong together, they stay face up, the team scores and goes again.
  3. If not, the cards turn back over and the next team plays. Remember where they were!
  4. When every pair is found, press New board. The boards take turns between two kinds of pairs.

Difficulty levels

LevelAudienceWhat changes
EasyPrimaryA picture and its word. 6 pairs, 1 point each.
ModerateLower secondaryWords that mean the same (big, large), then opposites (hot, cold). 8 pairs, 2 points each.
HardUpper secondaryWords that sound the same (knight, night), then idioms and their meanings (piece of cake, very easy). 6 pairs, 2 points each.

Scores

Use the +1 and −1 buttons to adjust any team. Podium shows the top three, and Send to Scoreboard passes the scores to the Scoreboard widget. Motion in the header turns the animations down or off.
