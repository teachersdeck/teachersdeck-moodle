Solar System Order

Science game

How to play

  1. The teacher picks a level, then presses Start round for the active team.
  2. Easy: tap the planets in order, starting nearest the Sun. Each right one glides into its orbit; all eight in order earns a +2 bonus and a new set.
  3. Moderate and Hard: read the clue and tap the planet it describes.
  4. Each right answer scores and builds a streak. When time is up, the next team plays.

Difficulty levels

LevelAudienceWhat changes
EasyPrimaryThe order of the eight planets from the Sun. 90 seconds, 1 point a planet.
ModerateLower secondaryPlanet facts: the largest, the hottest, the Red Planet, rings, storms. 2 points, a miss costs 1.
HardUpper secondaryMoons, size, orbits and light travel times. 3 points, a miss costs 1.

Scores

Use the +1 and −1 buttons to adjust any team. Podium shows the top three, and Send to Scoreboard passes the scores to the Scoreboard widget. Motion in the header turns the animations down or off; the Tiếng Việt button switches the game to Vietnamese.
